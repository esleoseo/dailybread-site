DailyBread Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

모티 프로젝트 ("we", "us") protects the personal information of users of the DailyBread app (the "Service") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws, and handles related requests promptly, as set out below.

Note: items marked "(planned)" refer to features not yet in the app. They apply from the time the feature is released.

The Korean version of this policy prevails if the two versions differ.

Voice input (speech recognition)

  • When you enter a verse by voice, the app uses Apple's speech recognition. Your voice may be sent to and processed on Apple's servers, under Apple's privacy policy. The recognized text is used only to check your input and is not stored on our servers.
  • You can turn off microphone and speech recognition permissions at any time in your device settings. Only voice input stops working; other input methods remain available.
  • If you do not consent to Share Voice in Team Reading, the recording is deleted from your device and never uploaded.

What we do not process

  • We do not collect national ID numbers, your contacts, your photo library or precise location.
  • Sharing to Instagram Stories happens on your device when you tap it, by handing an image to the Instagram app. We receive no information in that process.

Sensitive information

Reading records and life verses may reveal religious beliefs. We never ask about or separately collect your faith or religion; we use this content only to provide the Service (keeping your record, showing it to teammates) and never give it to ad companies or other third parties.

4. Advertising and personalized ads (planned)

  • To keep the Service free, we may show Google AdMob ads.
  • Before using the advertising identifier, the app asks for permission with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown, without the advertising identifier.
  • How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • We do not give your reading records, profile or voice recordings to ad companies.

5. Deleting your account

  • How: in the app, Settings → Account → Delete Account. If you no longer have the app, email esleoseo@gmail.com.
  • What is deleted: your account, profile, personal reading record, membership and team reading and poke records in every team, team items and your voice recording files are deleted immediately.
  • For teams you lead, leadership passes to another member; a team where you were the only member is deleted.
  • Paid items you bought are lost when you delete your account and cannot be restored. Refunds follow Apple's policy (see the Terms of Service).
  • If you signed up with Apple, you can also remove DailyBread under iPhone Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple.

6. Children under 14

  • The Service is intended for users aged 14 and over (App Store age rating) and does not accept sign-ups from children under 14. Only users aged 14 or over may sign in, and by signing in you agree to this policy and the Terms of Service.
  • If we learn that we have collected personal information of a child under 14, we delete it without delay. A legal guardian may request deletion through the contact in Section 9.
  • Look Around Without Signing In sends no personal information to us, so it can be used regardless of age.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • You may at any time request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information, or withdraw consent.
  • In the app: edit your profile (name, intro, life verse), turn off Share Voice in Team Reading (Settings), mute pokes (team settings), leave a team, delete your account.
  • For other requests, contact us at Section 9. We act without delay (within 10 days) and tell you the result.
  • You may make requests through an agent, who must submit a power of attorney.

8. Security measures

We take the following measures:

  • Communication between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Database access is restricted so that users can read only their own records and information about the teams they belong to.
  • Team voice recordings are kept in non-public storage and provided only to teammates through short-lived links.
  • Only the operator has server administration rights, and server admin secret keys are never included in the app or the source repository.
  • Changes to the server database are applied only after passing automated checks (tests).
